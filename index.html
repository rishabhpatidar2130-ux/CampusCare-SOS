Implementation Pathway

Deploying CampusCare Across LPU (Web Prototype to Native App)

Standard Operating Procedure and technical rollout blueprint for University Health Centre and Security operations.

1

Phase 1: Web-First Prototype

No app download required. Students scan corridor QR stickers and approve GPS access in 1 click. Zero barrier to entry during urgent emergencies.

2

Phase 2: Ambulance Tablets

Equip all 4 campus ambulances with dedicated touchscreen tablets loaded with the live dispatcher console and turn-by-turn campus GPS routing.

3

Phase 3: Native App & Panic Trigger

Triple-press power button hardware panic trigger. Automatic encrypted SMS fallback with GPS coordinates if Wi-Fi or 4G data is offline.

4

Phase 4: Health Records Integration

Securely stream vital medical data (blood group, allergies, asthma/diabetic history) to emergency responders as soon as the student triggers the SOS.
